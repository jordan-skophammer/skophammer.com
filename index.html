About
"Analog mindset, remote workflow."

After some time away from the industry, I'm back to help artists get their music to the finish line. My approach blends two worlds: real analog gear alongside modern in-the-box tools, so a mix keeps some grit and character without losing the speed and precision a digital workflow is built for.
